The Method

Both roads spend exactly the same money every month: your normal payment plus the extra. On the prepay road the extra kills principal; if the loan dies early, the whole freed payment rolls into investments for the rest of the hold. On the invest road the extra compounds at your expected return minus tax and fee drag. At the horizon we compare net positions — investments minus loan still owed. No home appreciation is counted: the house is the same house on both roads. If you itemize, the mortgage deduction quietly lowers the prepay road's guaranteed return, and the tool prices that too.

The plain rule: a 7% mortgage is a 7% guaranteed, tax-free return begging to be taken. A 3% mortgage is cheap leverage the market usually beats. The fight is in the middle — and that is exactly where the break-even return earns its keep.
